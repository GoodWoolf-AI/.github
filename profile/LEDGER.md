## A public record of collaborative AI training

The GoodWoolf Training Ledger is a public, community-maintained dataset for tracking collaborative model training.

It provides a shared place to contribute training updates, record model improvements, and make collaborative progress visible and accessible.

Rather than keeping training progress isolated across individual machines and contributors, the Training Ledger brings the community together around a shared record of model development.

It is part of the GoodWoolf project, built to make foundation model development more open, collaborative, and accessible.

<br />

## What is the Training Ledger?

The Training Ledger is designed to support decentralized and collaborative AI development.

Contributors can participate in building models by sharing training updates and documenting their contributions to the collective effort.

The ledger serves as a common reference point for the community, helping connect independent training efforts into a broader collaborative process.

**One community. Many contributors. Shared progress.**

<br />

## Core principles

**Open collaboration.**  
Make model training a collective effort rather than something limited to individual organizations or centralized infrastructure.

**Transparent progress.**  
Maintain a public record of training contributions and model development.

**Community participation.**  
Enable contributors to participate using their own hardware, resources, and available time.

**Shared improvements.**  
Bring individual contributions together to support the development of increasingly capable models.

**Open infrastructure.**  
Build on public datasets, open tooling, and community-driven development.

<br />

## How it fits into GoodWoolf

The Training Ledger is one of the core components of the GoodWoolf project.

| Component | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [GoodWoolf](https://github.com/GoodWoolf-AI/GoodWoolf) | Collaborative model training on commodity hardware |
| **Training Ledger** | Public record of collaborative training contributions |
| [GoodWoolf Docs](https://github.com/GoodWoolf-AI/GoodWoolf-Docs) | Documentation, guides, and usage examples |

Together, these projects provide the foundation for a more accessible and collaborative approach to building foundation models.
