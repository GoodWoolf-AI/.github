## Building foundation models collaboratively on commodity hardware

GoodWoolf is an open-source platform for building foundation models collaboratively on commodity hardware.

It combines local model training, virtual memory, and a public training ledger to make large-scale AI development more accessible.

The GoodWoolf ecosystem brings together the core platform, the Training Ledger, and documentation to provide a complete collaborative training environment.

## Why GoodWoolf?

**Open collaboration.**  
Make model training a collective effort rather than something limited to individual organizations.

**Own your infrastructure.**
Run locally or on your own servers, even with limited resources, at your own pace.

**Train the models you want.**
Train the models you choose, regardless of their size or computational demands.

**Train on your own data.**
Train on your own datasets and share models, not data.

**Stay open.**
GoodWoolf is developed publicly and designed around extensibility rather than vendor lock-in.
